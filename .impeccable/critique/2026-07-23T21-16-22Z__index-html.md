## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored for this product (8/10).** Three-font emotional register system, disciplined candlelit palette down to the seek-bar gradient, culturally fluent copy ("las palabras de papá y mamá hechas música", Corrido/Banda/Mariachi chips, "Ustedes elijan"), quinceañera correctly the hero tile, WhatsApp-first architecture, bespoke fallback engineering. Drifts generic at the stats row; hero film content unverified under portrait crop (16:9 frames cover-cropped to ~28% width on phones).

**Deterministic scan:** CLI: 1 advisory (13 em-dashes). Live scan: 31 findings — 10 dark-glow (mostly intentional brand candlelight; zero-offset glows fine), 16 undersized/tiny text (nav CTA 10.56px, SALTAR INTRO 10px, .occ-meta ×6 at 10.88px, kickers 11.84px, tier-badge 10.88px, quote-note 11.52px), 4 wide-tracking kickers, 6 repeated section kickers, thin-border+60px-shadow on tier-featured, width transition on flow progress. False positives: 9.92px "ESTUDIO" wordmark, all-caps genre metas (labels not body), .sticky clip (film mechanism).

**Mechanical evidence:** ~19MB transfer on load (182 image requests — the 179 hero frames); nav CTA tap target 143×29px, .occ-cta 33px tall; Google Fonts render-blocking; low-alpha microtext (.quote-note at 0.35 alpha ≈ ~3:1 contrast at 11.5px — AA fail); a11y mechanicals clean (all alts and accessible names present); real fixed elements: 4.

## Overall Impression

Top-quartile craft for a landing page: authored, culturally fluent, technically resilient (clean console, coherent degradation). The three worst problems cluster at the moment money changes hands: the creative brief never reaches the business, the deadline is never asked, and the journey ends on Stripe's generic receipt. The stage is beautiful; the box office is unstaffed.

## What's Working

1. **Fallback engineering as design integrity** — frames→generative scene, MP3→synth, Lenis→native, reduced-motion in five places; the site degrades into a coherent lesser version of itself.
2. **The occasion→package context thread** — tapping "Bodas" injects "— para su boda —" above pricing (aria-live); generic 3-tier table becomes personally addressed.
3. **Real mobile-first discipline** — verified bento collapse, Serenata reordered first, 52px sticky CTA with safe-area, 20px invisible touch zone on the 4px seek bar.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Paid orders arrive with no creative brief.** ORDER_ENDPOINT = "" (cinematic.js:626); flow answers live only in the customer's localStorage; Stripe links carry only client_reference_id. First customer = money with no song brief, and the WhatsApp recovery path is also a placeholder number. Fix: deploy orders-backend.gs, set the URL, and make POST failure non-silent (force the WhatsApp summary fallback). → /impeccable harden
2. **[P0] Dead contact surface.** wa.me/15555555555 on all four contact paths; Privacidad/Términos are href="#" while the FAQ promises confidentiality. Fix: real number + real policy pages before launch.
3. **[P1] No event date, no recap, before real money.** Deadline-critical product never asks "¿Cuándo es la fiesta?"; "Continuar al pago" fires with no summary of the four choices. Fix: date chip-step gating urgent dates to Gran Gala/WhatsApp; two-line recap opening step 4. → /impeccable shape
4. **[P1] ~17.5MB of hero frames.** ~3 min background download on 3G; a real cost on prepaid data plans common in the target market. Fix: recompress to ≤35KB/frame, cap mobile at ~60 frames, serve generative fallback on saveData/slow connections. → /impeccable optimize
5. **[P1] WhatsApp escape invisible where it matters.** At 360×640 the "¿Prefieres hablar primero?" link sits below the modal fold (y=561 vs clip 558) with no scroll affordance — the card-shy persona never sees it. Fix: move the WA link above the package list or into flow-nav. → /impeccable adapt
6. **[P2] The film hides the conversion apparatus.** Nav + sticky CTA hidden during the intro; only exits are the final overlay and 10px microtype. Fix: show CTA bar from ~50% film progress; "Saltar intro" ≥13px. → /impeccable adapt
7. **[P3] Featured-tier scale(1.04) silently overridden** by .reveal.in transform on desktop — "La más pedida" never pops. Fix: reveal transform on inner wrapper. → /impeccable polish

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** first viewport contains poetry + film chrome, zero product indication; comprehension arrives 3+ swipes in. "Crear mi canción" ambiguous (does he make it?) vs "Pedir mi canción".

**Casey (one-handed, 3G):** 17.5MB competes with her connection; sticky CTA (her thumb anchor) vanishes mid-film; modal × at top-right is worst reach zone. Win: flow state survives interruption.

**Riley (stress tester):** completed flow + reopened = lands directly on payment step where one tap fires Stripe redirect (happened twice in automation) — resume-into-live-payment with no recap is an ambush. No focus trap in aria-modal dialog; Escape closes mid-story without confirm; order POST swallowed by 4s Promise.race.

**Doña Marta (55, Spanish-dominant, budget Android, WhatsApp-primary):** can't parse 10px "SALTAR INTRO"; 29px nav CTA; WhatsApp link clipped below modal fold on her 360×640 — funneled toward card entry on a processor she's never heard of; tú register from a business reads careless to her generation in one card that says usted; 17.5MB vs prepaid data; 10.9px genre labels.

## Minor Observations

- Film in frames mode is a 16s time-based ping-pong, not scroll-scrubbed; motion reverses half the time.
- 16:9 frames cover-cropped to ~28% width on portrait; composition survival unverified.
- Three h1 elements in hero overlay.
- ESTRENO15 auto-applies at checkout but is invisible on the pricing page — a discount that never persuades.
- "3 días" (stats) vs "72 horas" (everywhere else).
- demo-note correctly auto-hides; instructional copy still ships in HTML.
- 4:26 full samples may be too generous — 60–90s hooks protect the asset and keep momentum.
- Low-alpha microtext fails AA (.quote-note ~3:1).
- Console completely clean across the session.

## Questions to Consider

1. You sell sound, but the crown jewel is a silent 17.5MB film. What if the scroll intro were the serenata itself — a verse that swells as you scroll, ending "así sonaría la suya"?
2. The story is the product's raw material — why is it optional, last, and 600 characters? What if the flow opened with "Cuéntanos de ella" and packages came after emotional investment?
3. Tú or usted? The buyer with the money (Doña Marta) and the buyer in the copy are different people. That one register choice does more trust work than the entire stats section.
